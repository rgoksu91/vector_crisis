Oyun hakkında

Her level, farklı davranan oklarla dolu bir grid'dir. Normal bir ok yalnızca baktığı yönde önü boşsa çıkar; rotator ok kapalıysa döner; donmuş ok komşusu kalkınca çözülür; bomba ok çıkarken çevresini temizler; taş ok ise çıkmaz — kaydığı yerde kalıcı duvara dönüşür ve yanlış zamanda dokunulursa bir çıkışı tamamen kapatabilir. Dişli ok her hamlede döndüğü için sabit bir rutin işe yaramaz. Level 4'ten itibaren her dokunuş hamle bütçenden düşer; üç yıldız için en kısa çözümü bulman gerekir.

Vector Crisis: Arrow Puzzle

A strategy puzzle about clearing the board by sending out the arrows whose path is free — and about getting the order right.

About the game

Every level is a grid of arrows that each behave differently. A plain arrow leaves only if nothing blocks the direction it faces; a rotator turns when its way is shut; a frozen arrow thaws once a neighbour is gone; a bomb clears its surroundings on the way out; a stone never leaves at all — it slides, hardens into a permanent wall, and can seal off an exit for good if you touch it at the wrong moment. A gear arrow turns after every move, so no fixed routine works. From level 4 on, every tap costs you from a move budget, and three stars require the shortest solution.

  • A 300-level campaign that keeps getting harder
  • Six arrow types with rules that interlock
  • English and Turkish interface
  • Free, with no in-app purchases
  • Playable offline — a connection is needed only for ads
