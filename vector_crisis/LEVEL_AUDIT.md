# Vector Crisis: Arrow Puzzle — 300-Level Audit

Audit source: `dart run tool/level_audit.dart` (per-level CSV plus the summary
below). Levels 1–3 are hand-made tutorials; levels 4–300 are generated by
`tool/generate_campaign.sh` from the rules in `tool/level_plan.dart`.

## Final result


Every `targetMoves` equals the solver optimum, and every optimal solution is
replayed against an independent gameplay simulation in
`test/game/levels_test.dart`. The tutorial layouts are protected by the
fingerprint `1018013165`.

## Difficulty by band

The difficulty model (`lib/game/logic/level_difficulty.dart`) plays each level
240 times per player type:

- **Unplanned** taps anything that can exit, and otherwise anything that reacts.
- **Careful** plays the same way but refuses to slide a Stone onto a ray that is
  open right now — one move of lookahead.

A run fails if it jams the board or overruns the move limit.

| Levels | Optimal moves | Unplanned 3★ | Unplanned fail | Unplanned jam | Careful fail | Careful 3★ |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4–11 | 5–7 | 62% | 20% | 0% | 20% | 62% |
| 12–50 | 7–20 | 1% | 90% | 83% | 82% | 2% |
| 51–100 | 20–31 | 1% | 95% | 92% | 92% | 3% |
| 101–150 | 32–33 | 0% | 99% | 97% | 98% | 0% |
| 151–200 | 33 | 0% | 99% | 99% | 95% | 1% |
| 201–250 | 35 | 0% | 99% | 98% | 97% | 0% |
| 251–300 | 37 | 0% | 100% | 99% | 98% | 1% |

Levels 4–11 are the mechanic introductions and are meant to be easy. From the
first Stone on, clearing a level needs planning more than one move ahead. These
are simulated players, not play-test data. The bands from level 12 on may prove
too harsh and are the first thing to check with real players.

## Pacing

- Challenge levels: every tenth level (10, 20, …, 300)
- Highest minimum solution: Level 251 — 37 moves
- Most special arrows: Level 202 — 16
- Densest board: Level 120 — 62.5% of cells
- Most exits on move one: Level 51 — 6
- Total optimal moves across the catalog: 8,839
- Move budget: `targetMoves + moveSlack`, where `moveSlack` is 2–4 depending on
  level length; three stars require the optimum

The optimum grows quickly up to level 100 and then flattens. Past that point,
difficulty comes from Stone traps, not from more taps.

## Gameplay/solver parity

- The solver, the difficulty model and the generator share one transition
  function, `BoardEngine.move`.
- A state key holds the arrows still on the board, two bits of turn per
  Rotator and three bits per Stone recording where it landed.
- A Stone slides to the first arrow, wall or edge and becomes a permanent wall.
  It cannot move with no free cell ahead, and bombs cannot blast it.
- Frozen arrows thaw when an orthogonal neighbour leaves its cell, a sliding
  Stone included.
- A bomb removed as a victim does not chain-trigger.
- In the game, a position with landed Stones is passed to the solver through
  `LevelData.walls`. The hint follows the solver's next move. On Stone levels
  a background check ends the run as soon as the board can no longer be
  cleared.

## Recommended manual test set

```text
6, 8, 10, 12, 13, 20, 30, 51, 100, 101, 150, 200, 251, 281, 300
```

Level 12 introduces Stones, 51 is the first 8x8 board and 281 has the only
mid-solution forced run. The century levels mark the flattening of the
optimum.
