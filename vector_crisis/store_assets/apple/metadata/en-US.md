Every arrow has a way out—but only if you choose the right order.

Vector Crisis is a precision logic puzzle where every move reshapes the board. Read each path, uncover dependencies, and clear every arrow without wasting a move. What starts simple grows into layered puzzles built around timing, space, and consequence.

MASTER FIVE ARROW TYPES

Normal arrows test your path reading. Rotators change direction. Frozen arrows need to be freed. Bombs transform the board. Stones slide into place and become walls. Learn how they interact, then combine them to solve deeper challenges.

## App Review notes

Vector Crisis does not require an account or login. The game opens directly to the main menu.

The app uses Google Mobile Ads. Interstitial ads appear only at natural level transitions and are rate-limited. Rewarded ads are optional and can grant three extra moves or additional hints. Core gameplay does not require watching a rewarded ad.

No special hardware, external account, or review credentials are required.
